# Night trains with a pass (Benelux and France)

Checked 25 Oct 2026 for the 2026/27 timetable (starts 13 Dec 2026). Operating
days, times and holiday running change per timetable; always check the
operator's timetable for the actual date.

Travel-day rule (CoU 11.1.1): one travel day for a direct night train with no
change after midnight; never on the last valid day; earlier trains on the
departure day are on the same travel day. Reservations are mandatory on all
night trains, berths cost more than seats, and a 1st class pass gives no
discount except the French 1st class 4-berth couchette, priced like 2nd class
(https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/reservation-fees/night-train-reservation-fees).

**There is no direct sleeper from the Netherlands to the south of France or
Spain in winter 2026/27.** European Sleeper's Brussels-Barcelona train is
planned for 2028 (https://www.europeansleeper.eu/barcelona).

## European Sleeper

- Brussels - Cologne - Switzerland - Milan: runs since 9 Sep 2026, three times a
  week; from 13 Dec 2026 also stops in Antwerp, Breda and Eindhoven
  (https://www.europeansleeper.eu/milan,
  https://www.europeansleeper.eu/night-train-netherlands-milan). 2026 pattern:
  from Brussels Mon/Thu/Sat, from Milan Wed/Fri/Sun
  (https://www.europeansleeper.eu/timetable). Sample: Brussels 18:17 -
  Milano Porta Garibaldi 11:45. December times not yet published.
- Brussels - Amsterdam - Berlin - Prague and Paris - Brussels - Berlin: three
  times a week each; eastbound, so not useful toward Spain.
- Global Pass accepted, both classes; no domestic-only journeys
  (https://www.europeansleeper.eu/interrail).
- Fixed pass fares: Budget seat EUR 11-21, Classic berth EUR 64-74, Comfort
  sleeper from EUR 79 (triple) to EUR 179 (Plus single). Private compartment:
  every occupant needs a pass.
- Book on europeansleeper.eu ("Interrail reservation"), via Interrail (EUR 2) or
  SNCB b-europe. Refund 100% up to 30 days, 50% up to 15 days before.

## OBB Nightjet from the Netherlands

- Daily Amsterdam/Utrecht - Munich - Vienna/Innsbruck and Amsterdam -
  Basel/Zurich (https://www.nightjet.com/en/reiseziele/niederlande/amsterdam,
  https://www.nsinternational.com/en/trains/nighttrain). New trains on
  NL-Basel/Zurich from 13 Dec 2026 (NS).
- Pass accepted with a paid reservation per person; pass quota limited per train
  (https://www.nightjet.com/en/buchung/tarifinformationen/interrail-eurail-paesse).
- Supplements vary with demand: seat from EUR 4.90, 6-berth couchette from
  EUR 19.90, 4-berth from EUR 29.90, sleeper from EUR 59.90.
- Book on nightjet.com with the "Interrail/Eurail - Globalpass" discount, OBB
  phone +43 5 1717, Interrail or SBB. Free cancellation up to 15 days before.

## France: Intercités de Nuit (from Paris Austerlitz)

| Route | Runs |
|---|---|
| Paris - Toulouse | daily |
| Paris - Foix - Latour-de-Carol | daily |
| Paris - Nîmes - Montpellier - Perpignan - Cerbère | daily in summer, on some days the rest of the year (SNCF Connect showed a 26 Oct 2026 run, Austerlitz 21:25 - Cerbère 09:16); check the date |
| Paris - Bayonne - Pau - Lourdes - Tarbes | daily |
| Paris - Marseille - Toulon - Cannes - Nice | daily |
| Paris - Rodez - Albi, Paris - Aurillac, Paris - Briançon | not daily |

Source: https://www.interrail.com/en/plan-your-trip/tips-and-tricks/trains-europe/night-trains/intercites-de-nuit

- Mandatory reservation, bookable up to 90 days ahead, limited pass places.
- Pass fees: seat about EUR 10-11, couchette (2nd 6-berth or 1st 4-berth) about
  EUR 19-25 (two Interrail pages differ slightly).
- Booking: SNCF phone +33 1 84 94 36 35 (#85 English), SNCB b-europe, Rail
  Europe; Interrail's own pages disagree on whether its service sells them.
- From Latour-de-Carol, Rodalies continues to Barcelona; no reservation needed
  on Rodalies.
- Combination: Eurostar or day train to Paris, then the night train, is one
  travel day.

## Not usable with a pass

- Travelski Night Express (Amsterdam - Tarentaise, Fridays): its site and terms
  never mention passes; separate ticket via Travelski only
  (https://www.nl.travelski.com/vervoer/nachttrein-wintersport).
- Eurostar Snow/Sun trains.
