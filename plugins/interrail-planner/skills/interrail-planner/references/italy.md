# Italy and the Riviera border (Italy-France)

Checked 25 Oct 2026, for travel Dec 2026 - Jan 2027. Official sources only;
fees are Interrail's indicative pass-holder fees. The Italian and French winter
timetables start 13 Dec 2026. Every timetable read here (Trenitalia, Trenitalia
France) runs only until 12 Dec 2026, so check train times again after the
change.

- Sources: https://www.interrail.com/en/plan-your-trip/tips-and-tricks/trains-europe/railway-companies
  and https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/reservation-fees/domestic-train-reservation-fees
- Trenitalia's own conditions: pass holders need a "Global Pass" ticket that
  includes the mandatory reservation on Frecce, IC, ICN, domestic EC and
  FrecciaLink. **Executive, Premium and Cabine Superior are excluded**, so a
  1st-class pass gets Business (Frecce) or 1st (IC) at most
  (https://www.trenitalia.com/content/dam/trenitalia/allegati/info/condizioni-generali-di-trasporto/parte-ii-trasporto-nazionale/Parte_II-Trasporto_Nazionale.pdf).
- Buying on board is normally not allowed. Without a valid ticket you pay the
  full fare plus a penalty (same document, ch. 1 points 2.6 and 7.1).
- Leonardo Express (Roma-Fiumicino): 1st class only, so it is fine with a 1st-class pass
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/plan-your-trip/tips-and-tricks/trains-europe/trains-country/trains-italy).
- Quotas: none published by Trenitalia or Interrail.

## Booking channels and window

- Interrail reservation service: EUR 2 per person per train, plus EUR 9 per order
  for paper tickets. The page also lists the Trenitalia call centre, Italiarail
  ("I have an InterRail Pass"; not all classes), stations in Italy, and ÖBB, Rail
  Europe and Happyrail. Freccialink is bookable only through Happyrail
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/how-do-i-book-seats).
- Trenitalia: Global Pass tickets are sold at ticket offices and authorised agencies.
  Self-service machines and lefrecce.it/the app are **not mentioned** for
  pass holders (Parte II above). Trenitalia's pass page sends you to interrail.eu
  (https://www.trenitalia.com/it/abbonamenti-pass-e-carnet/pass-interrail.html).
- Station bookings: EUR 2 local fee, 80% refundable before departure (domestic fee page).
- Trenitalia call centre: +39 06 5210550, option 1 for English
  (https://help.interrail.com/hc/en-001/articles/15873067026205-Contacting-the-railway-companies).
- Window: up to 2 months ahead, 3 months for some trains
  (https://www.trenitalia.com/en/offers/faq-interrail.html).

## Milano - Genova - Ventimiglia

- Interrail route advice: IC Milano-Genova-Ventimiglia, then a regional train to Monaco/Nice
  with a change at Ventimiglia (Italy country page above).
- Ventimiglia-Milano Centrale direct trains on RFI's autumn table: IC 655 at 04:51,
  IC 745 at 11:03, IC 1539 at 13:03, FB 905 at 15:10, IC 1537 at 17:03 and
  FB 911 at 19:43. About 3h45-4h10 (the table covers 25 Sep-24 Oct, works
  version) (https://www.rfi.it/content/dam/rfi/news-e-media/infomobilit%C3%A0/poq-genova/poq-genova-2/LH%20Ventimiglia%20-%20Milano.pdf).
  So: about 6 direct IC/Frecciabianca trains a day, each needing a reservation
  (EUR 3 IC, EUR 10 FB).
- Milano-Genova is also served by Regionale Veloce trains (no reservation), e.g.
  RV 3013/3025 Milano C.le-Genova Brignole
  (https://www.rfi.it/it/news-e-media/infomobilita/avvisi/2026/9/15/linee--genova---milano.html).
  Genova-Ventimiglia regional trains: niet bevestigd op officiële bron.
- A Frecciarossa Milano-Genova: niet bevestigd op officiële bron.

## Ventimiglia - Menton - Monaco - Nice

- TER (ZOU!) regional trains, pass valid, **no reservation**
  (railway-companies page, "Transdev ZOU! ... no reservation").
- Ventimiglia-Nice: about 31 trains a day, about 1h, 05:15-21:08 (sample day)
  (https://www.sncf-connect.com/en-en/train/timetables/ventimiglia/nice).
  Nice-Menton has 65 round trips a day and takes about 40 min
  (https://www.ter.sncf.com/sud-provence-alpes-cote-d-azur/trajet-nice-menton).
- Works: no trains between Nice and Ventimiglia on 10 Nov 2026 and until about
  17:30 on 11 Nov. Nothing is listed for Dec-Jan
  (https://www.ter.sncf.com/sud-provence-alpes-cote-d-azur/se-deplacer/info-trafic/travaux/nice-monaco-vintimille-nice-breil-tende).
- Trenitalia trains running through Ventimiglia-Nice: niet bevestigd op officiële bron.
- Through trains Milano-Nice or Milano-Marseille: none found. SNCF Connect
  shows Milan-Nice only via Paris (about 14 h)
  (https://www.sncf-connect.com/en-en/train/timetables/milan/nice). The
  Riviera route means changing at Ventimiglia. No successor to Thello appears on
  any official source.

## Milano - Torino - Lyon/Paris

- The Maurienne line reopened on 31 Mar 2025. TGV INOUI Paris-Turin-Milan has 3
  round trips a day (https://www.sncf-voyageurs.com/fr/presse/sncf-voyageurs-relance-sa-liaison-directe-en-tgv-inoui-entre-paris-les-alpes-turin-et-milan-des-aujourdhui/).
  Paris-Milan takes about 7 h (https://www.sncf-connect.com/en-en/tgv-inoui-italy).
- Frecciarossa (Trenitalia France) runs 2 round trips a day Paris-Lyon Part-Dieu-
  Chambéry-Modane-Torino-Milano, on a timetable valid to 12 Dec 2026
  (https://trenitalia.fr/voyager-avec-trenitalia/nos-trains-et-lignes/les-horaires/).
  Trenitalia France's traffic page (updated 14 Oct 2026) lists no Maurienne closure
  for winter (https://trenitalia.fr/info-trafic/).
- Pass fees, both mandatory: Frecciarossa Milan-Turin-Lyon-Paris EUR 13 (Standard/Business);
  TGV Milan-Turin-Paris EUR 45 (1st) / 31 (2nd)
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/reservation-fees/international-train-reservation-fees).
- Frecciarossa Paris-Lyon-Marseille is valid for domestic French journeys only (railway-companies page).

## Night trains (Intercity Notte)

- Routes: Torino-Salerno/Reggio Calabria/Lecce, Milano-Lecce, Milano-Sicilia,
  Roma-Sicilia/Trieste and others. 24 trains a day in total
  (https://www.trenitalia.com/en/connections/intercity-notte.html). None run from
  Genova or Ventimiglia: niet bevestigd op officiële bron.
- Pass fees: seat EUR 3, 4-berth couchette EUR 41, sleeper EUR 48 (3-berth),
  EUR 58 (double) or EUR 122 (single). The Superior cabin is excluded
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/reservation-fees/night-train-reservation-fees).

## Practical

- Mobile pass: add the journey in Rail Planner before boarding. Inspectors scan the
  barcode and check your ID (original only). The barcode opens gates at Italian stations;
  if a gate does not open, ask staff (https://www.trenitalia.com/content/dam/trenitalia/allegati/offerte/interrail/IT-Interrail_Pass_Guide_2025.pdf;
  https://help.interrail.com/hc/en-001/articles/9047394672413-Station-gates-How-to-Pass).
  Stamping regional tickets does not apply to a pass.
- Strikes: on weekdays regional trains are guaranteed 06:00-09:00 and 18:00-21:00.
  Guaranteed long-distance trains are in "Tabella A"
  (https://www.trenitalia.com/it/informazioni/treni-garantiti-incasodisciopero.html).
  The Christmas exemption period is not stated there: niet bevestigd op officiële bron.
