# Seat reservations for pass holders, by country

Checked 25 Oct 2026. Fees are Interrail's indicative pass-holder fees and change
often; re-check before quoting. Main fee pages:
- domestic: https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/reservation-fees/domestic-train-reservation-fees
- international: https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/reservation-fees/international-train-reservation-fees
- night trains: https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/reservation-fees/night-train-reservation-fees

## Rules everywhere

- Reservations are not included in the pass. Mandatory on most high-speed
  trains (TGV, Eurostar, Lyria, Frecce, AVE), all night-train berths and
  panoramic coaches (CoU 8.1.3). A reservation alone is not a ticket (CoU 8.1.4).
- Pass-holder seats may be limited on some trains (CoU 8.1.5). No operator
  publishes quota sizes. When sold out: another train, a full-fare ticket from
  the operator, or the timetable's "No reservation needed" filter
  (https://help.interrail.com/hc/en-001/articles/11443212369437-Limited-Seat-Availability).
- Interrail reservation service: EUR 2 per person per train, plus EUR 9 per
  order for paper tickets. Book on the website, logged in with the pass
  (activation not needed); the help centre says the app only links to the
  website (an older app page still claims in-app booking for some routes)
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/how-do-i-book-seats).
- Booking windows: many high-speed and night trains open about 3 months ahead;
  Interrail also says 3-6 months for most trains. Book festive periods as early
  as possible. Individual pages contradict each other (see below); check the
  real opening in the Interrail timetable or the operator's sales calendar.
- Most reservations are non-refundable; the booking fee never is. Renfe AVE,
  Euromed and Alvia can be changed once (date/time), at least 48 h before
  departure via customer service
  (https://help.interrail.com/hc/en-001/articles/11204728547485-Are-my-reservations-exchangeable).

## Netherlands and Belgium

- NS domestic trains covered (and Arriva, Keolis, Qbuzz etc. on domestic
  routes). Not covered: Go Volta, Eurostar Snow, Arriva night train.
- NS International: ICE seats online for pass holders; phone bookings
  EUR 7.50 per person, max EUR 22.50 per order
  (https://www.nsinternational.com/en/buying-tickets/seat-reservation-ice).
- Belgian station booking fee for international trains: EUR 7 (ICE),
  EUR 12 (Eurostar, TGV, European Sleeper).

## Eurostar

- Mandatory reservation, limited pass-holder fare; sold out means full fare
  (https://www.eurostar.com/rw-en/deals/interrail).
- 1st class pass: Standard or Plus; Premier not available.
- Not bookable on eurostar.com online; use Interrail, NS International, SNCB/SNCF
  counters or Eurostar's pass-fare phone line (+33 8 25 84 25 97).
- Opens up to 6 months ahead (Interrail). Exchange: Interrail says none,
  Eurostar says one exchange before departure (sources differ).

## France

- Pass valid on TER, Intercités, TGV INOUI, Intercités de Nuit, ZOU!. Not on
  RER, Transilien, Metro, Chemins de fer de Provence/Corse, Euskotren.
- **OUIGO conflict:** Interrail lists OUIGO as covered; SNCF Connect's Interrail
  page says the pass covers compulsory-reservation trains "except OUIGO"
  (https://www.sncf-connect.com/en-en/catalogue/description/interrail-pass).
  Interrail issues the pass, so its list weighs more; still check the France
  network guide in Rail Planner and prefer TGV INOUI when there is a choice.
- Booking window: Interrail France page says about 2 months, TGV page up to 6
  months. SNCF's own sales calendar is the practical answer: winter holiday
  sales (13 Dec 2026 - 6 Jan 2027) opened 30 Sep 2026; TGV to Spain on sale up
  to 31 Mar 2027 (https://www.sncf-connect.com/aide/l-ouverture-des-ventes).
- No pass-holder booking on SNCF Connect is confirmed; SNCF phone #85 (English,
  08:00-20:00) works.
- Which Intercités day lines need a reservation is not readable from SNCF's
  map; check per train.

## Spain

- Pass valid on AVE, Alvia, Avant, Euromed, Intercity, Media Distancia,
  Cercanías/Rodalies, FEVE, iryo. Not valid on Avlo, OUIGO España, Euskotren,
  FGC, FGV, tourist/luxury trains.
- Station booking window: one page says up to one year, the AVE page 3 months.
  International AVE: up to one year.
- Renfe phone pre-reservation +34 91 232 03 20: closes 24 h before departure,
  must be paid and collected at a station within 72 h showing the pass.
- No pass-holder fare on renfe.com or the Renfe app is confirmed.
- Andalusia: Madrid-Córdoba-Sevilla/Granada/Málaga and Córdoba-Sevilla/Granada
  run AVE and long-distance trains (reservation needed); Avlo also runs some of
  these and is not valid
  (https://www.renfe.com/es/es/inspirate/rutas/cordoba-sevilla,
  https://www.renfe.com/es/es/inspirate/rutas/sevilla-granada,
  https://www.renfe.com/es/es/inspirate/rutas/cordoba-granada).
- Sevilla-Granada: in 2026 storm damage around Antequera led to bus
  replacements and fewer Sevilla-Córdoba-Granada trains
  (https://grupo.renfe.com/es/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/2026/02/renfe-ajusta-horarios-avant-sevilla-malaga).
  Check the actual date; fallback is via Córdoba (two AVE legs, same travel day).
- Expect high demand Madrid-Andalucía around Christmas; Renfe usually adds
  capacity but publishes no holiday cuts in advance.

## Germany, Switzerland, Italy

- Italy and the Riviera crossing in detail: `italy.md`.

- DB: reservations bookable up to 6 months (some 3)
  (https://int.bahn.de/en/faq/reservation-interrail).
- Trenitalia: Frecce reservations up to 2 months (3 for some trains)
  (https://www.trenitalia.com/en/offers/faq-interrail.html).
- SBB: optional IC reservations; panorama trains via the operator.
