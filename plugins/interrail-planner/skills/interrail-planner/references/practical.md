# Practical rules on the road

Checked 25 Oct 2026. Sources: Interrail CoU v19, help.interrail.com,
interrail.com support pages, operator sites, EU Regulation 2021/782.

## Rail Planner app checklist

- Before the first train of a travel day: activate the travel day, then add
  every leg (My Trip toggle). Both need a stable connection - do it on station
  or hotel Wi-Fi (CoU 9.3.1, 9.4.1, 9.6.1).
- Ticket: My Pass > travel day > "Show ticket". One barcode per day, valid all
  day; night-train legs stay on the departure day's ticket
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/support/already-bought-pass/mobile-pass-ticket-inspection).
- Open the app online once every 24 hours or the pass goes inactive (CoU 9.6.2).
- Train missing in the planner: try the local station name, else add manually;
  for a delayed train enter the scheduled departure time.
- Barcodes are scanned in e.g. France, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria
  and (except regional trains) Germany; Spain and Belgium check visually. Carry
  ID. Show the reservation as well.
- No screenshots. Keep the phone charged (power bank). Keep the pass number
  and order email off the phone: after loss use "Move Pass" on a new device;
  saved trips are lost and must be re-added
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/support/already-bought-pass/what-should-i-do-if-my-pass-is-lost-or-stolen).

## Delays and missed connections

- Ask staff first; they can rebook. Interrail chat in the app (More > Chat),
  07:00-23:59.
- If nobody offers an alternative within 100 minutes you may arrange rail or
  bus transport yourself and claim necessary costs (not taxi or flights)
  (https://help.interrail.com/hc/en-001/articles/9565493365149-Assistance-during-train-delays-or-cancellations;
  Reg. 2021/782 art. 18).
- **Interrail does not reimburse missed reservations.** A leg with a
  mandatory reservation is a separate contract, not a through-ticket under
  2021/782 (CoU 3.4.2). Changing the reservation at a station may cost a fee.
  Plan at least 20 minutes per transfer, more before a reserved train
  (https://help.interrail.com/hc/en-001/articles/17844156204573-Short-transfer-time).
- Compensation: EUR 12 (60-119 min) / EUR 24 (120+ min), max 50% of the pass
  price, claim within 3 months after validity (CoU 13.1). Keep proof of delay.
- Under 2021/782 art. 20: meals, and a hotel if an overnight stay becomes
  necessary, for delays of 60+ minutes.

## When pass-holder seats are sold out

- Another train, another class (a 1st class pass may book 2nd class), a
  train without mandatory reservation ("No reservation needed" filter), another
  channel (operator phone, station desk), or a full-fare ticket.
- Alternatives to the busy Paris-Barcelona TGV: night train to
  Latour-de-Carol then Rodalies, night train to Cerbère (daily in summer,
  some days otherwise), or TGV to Perpignan plus TER to Portbou
  (about 50 minutes, no reservation stated).

## Reservations bought from Interrail

- PDF e-ticket by email, also in the account; print it if the ticket says so.
- Mostly non-refundable; booking fee never refunded. Renfe AVE/Euromed/Alvia:
  one date/time change; certain French TGVs: one change; Eurostar via its own
  booking management. Contact at least 48 h before departure
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/terms-conditions/seat-reservation-booking-conditions).

## Luggage

- Renfe long distance: up to 3 pieces, 25 kg in total; tags mandatory on AVE
  International (https://www.renfe.com/es/en/travel/informacion-util/luggage/politica-de-equipajes).
- SNCF TGV INOUI/Intercités: 2 suitcases + 1 small bag, all labelled; fines
  EUR 50-150 (https://www.sncf-connect.com/en-en/help/transport-luggage).

## Holidays and peaks

- Book as early as possible in festive periods; Paris-Barcelona is named as a
  route that sells out
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/book-reservations/all-about-seat-reservations).
- Holiday timetables (24/25/31 Dec, 1 Jan) are not announced far ahead; check
  the operator's journey planner close to the date.
- Strikes announced 48 h or more ahead: extra costs generally not reimbursed.
