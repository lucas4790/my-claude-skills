# Interrail pass rules

Checked 25 Oct 2026 against the official sources. Main legal source: Interrail
Pass Conditions of Use v19, in force 10 Sep 2026 ("CoU"):
https://www.interrail.com/content/dam/_new-structure/doc/terms/v19_interrail_pass_conditions_of_use.pdf
interrail.eu redirects to interrail.com. The conditions that apply are those
published when the pass was bought, unless a later version is more favourable
(CoU 2.2.2).

## Pass types

- Global Pass: all 33 countries, all services of participating companies, with
  the home-country limit below (CoU 4.2.1, 4.3.1).
- Flexi: a fixed number of travel days within a validity period. Continuous:
  every day of the period is a travel day (CoU 4.6).
- Global Plus Pass: also covers most seat reservations, booked with credits
  (CoU 8.1.2; https://www.interrail.com/en/interrail-passes/global-pass).
- Which companies take part: Rail Planner > More > Rail Network Guides is
  authoritative; Appendix 1 of the CoU is not exhaustive (CoU 4.2.1). A leg on a
  non-participating operator must be paid at the normal fare (CoU 4.2.2).

## Travel day and validity

- A travel day is one calendar day, 00:00 to 23:59:59 local time (CoU 4.10.1).
  Crossing a time zone: local time applies (CoU 4.10.3).
- Validity runs from 00:00 on the start date to 23:59:59 on the final date
  (CoU 4.9.2). Travel days only within validity (CoU 4.10.2).
- Counting the period: Interrail's example, 1 month from 10 Jan is valid up to
  and including 9 Feb
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/support/interested-in-interrailing/how-long-is-my-interrail-pass-valid).
  A 2-month pass from 21 Dec therefore runs to 20 Feb (inferred from that
  example; the app shows the real dates under My Pass).

## Night trains

- A direct overnight train or ferry uses one travel day, the departure day, if
  there is no change of train after midnight and the arrival day is within
  validity. Changing after midnight uses a second travel day (CoU 11.1.1).
- **There is no 19:00 / "7pm" rule** in current official terms. Interrail's 2026
  Pass Guide example departs at 18:10
  (https://www.interrail.com/content/dam/_new-structure/doc/pass-guides/pass-guides-2026/interrail_pass_guide_2026.pdf).
- Trains taken earlier on the departure day are on the same travel day
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/plan-your-trip/tips-and-tricks/trains-europe/night-trains).
- A night train cannot depart on the last day of validity
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/support/interested-in-interrailing/what-is-a-travel-day).
- The night journey sits on the departure day's ticket; show that ticket after
  midnight too.
- Couchettes and beds are always booked and paid separately (CoU 8.1.3).

## Home country (country of residence)

- Only two journeys in the country of residence: one outbound (to the border,
  an airport or a port) and one inbound (back from there) (CoU 4.3.2).
- Each may use several trains if they are all on the same travel day (CoU 4.3.2).
- They use normal travel days, not extra ones (CoU 4.3.3). Other domestic use
  is not allowed (CoU 4.3.4).
- Order and timing are free; transiting through the home country counts as one
  of the two (https://www.interrail.com/en/interrail-passes/what-is-interrail/travel-your-own-country).
- Eurostar and European Sleeper need the pass valid in both countries, so
  leaving the home country on them uses the outbound journey (CoU Appendix 1).
- Night train home: add two journeys, to the last border station on day 1 and
  from the border on day 2; the inbound journey is day 2, so two travel days
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/interrail-passes/interrail-mobile-pass/mobile-pass-faq/how-can-i-travel-back-home-by-night-train).
- Residence = where you live on the first day of validity (CoU 5.1).
- Using the outbound/inbound journeys is optional ("not obliged"; mobile pass
  FAQ, how-can-I-travel-in-my-country-of-residence-with-a-global-pass).
  Covering the home-country leg with a separate domestic ticket therefore leaves
  that journey unused - an inference, no official page covers the case
  explicitly. It does **not** save a travel day if you continue on the pass the
  same calendar day.

## Activation (mobile pass)

- Three steps: activate the pass, activate each travel day (Flexi), add every
  journey (CoU 9.1.1).
- Start date must be within 11 months of purchase (CoU 4.9.4). It can be
  changed until 00:00 on that date by deactivating and reactivating (CoU 9.2.2).
- A travel day can be cancelled until 00:00 on that day; after that it is used
  (CoU 9.3.1).
- The pass counts as used once validity has started (CoU 4.9.3).

## Mobile pass in use

- Every journey and every leg must be added in Rail Planner before boarding;
  otherwise there is no valid ticket (CoU 9.4.1). No official grace procedure for
  adding on board.
- Activating days and adding journeys needs a stable internet connection
  (CoU 9.6.1). Afterwards the ticket can be shown offline, but the app must be
  opened online at least once every 24 hours or the pass becomes inactive until
  it is (CoU 9.6.2). One FAQ mentions 3 days; follow the CoU.
- Screenshots, photos or copies are not accepted (CoU 12.1.2). The phone must
  stay on, charged and readable (CoU 12.1.3). One device at a time; iOS 16+ or
  Android 8+ (CoU 7.1.2, 7.3.1).
- Without a valid ticket: full fare and/or fine; pay first, dispute later via
  customer service (CoU 12.2).

## Class

- A 1st class pass is valid in 1st and 2nd class; no refund when travelling in
  2nd (CoU 4.7). If 1st class reservations are sold out, a 2nd class
  reservation may be booked (Pass Guide 2026).

## Refund and exchange

- Only before activation (or after deactivating a pass whose start date has not
  begun). Refund policy V12 (10 Sep 2026):
  https://www.interrail.com/en/interrail-passes/ordering-info/refund-exchange-policy
  - within 7 days of purchase: full;
  - after 7 days with Refund Protection: full, no exchange fee;
  - after 7 days without: 10% fee (refund) or EUR 25 (exchange).
- Refund Protection can only be added at checkout
  (https://www.interrail.com/en/interrail-passes/ordering-info/secure-your-pass).
- No statutory cooling-off period (CoU 17.5.1).

## Delay compensation

- EUR 12 for 60-119 minutes, EUR 24 for 120+ minutes late at the final
  destination of a contract of carriage; capped at 50% of the pass price; claim
  within 3 months after validity ends (CoU 13.1).
- Interrail does not reimburse missed reservations; see `practical.md`.
